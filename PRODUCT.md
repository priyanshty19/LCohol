# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

21+ adults in India interested in drinking culture — cocktails, bars, spirits, nightlife — who want a social space without attaching their real identity to it. They arrive pseudonymously (often via referral), browse and share "sip stories," discover drinks and bars, mix cocktails, and lean on James, the in-app AI bartender. Context of use is casual and social: evenings, weekends, often on mobile, often a drink already in hand.

## Product Purpose

SIPSTORIES is an anonymous social platform for India's drinking culture. It is explicitly **not** a marketplace or delivery app — no purchase links, no real prices, no alcohol sales. It exists to let people share experiences, discover drinks and venues, and get bartender-quality guidance, all under a pseudonym and within Indian legal/compliance guardrails (21+ age gate, prohibition-state geo-disclaimers, responsible-drinking messaging, IT Act safe harbor). Success looks like a visitor feeling they've stepped into a warm, members-only lounge and wanting in.

## Brand Personality

Speakeasy warmth. Three words: **suave, intimate, characterful.** The voice is the house bartender James: charming, quick-witted, light Hinglish, India-fluent, never corporate. The "Midnight Lounge" design language (deep velvet/burgundy dark mode, an Ivory Cream light mode, glass / gold / velvet materials, Playfair display serif) should feel like a dim, well-appointed bar — inviting, adult, a little secret — not a tech product or a delivery app. Emotional goal: belonging and anticipation.

## Anti-references

- **Delivery / marketplace apps** (Swiggy/Zomato/liquor-delivery): no carts, prices, "order now," or transactional energy.
- **Generic SaaS landing pages**: no hero-metric template (big-number + stat row), no identical icon-card grids, no tiny uppercase eyebrows above every section.
- **Loud nightclub / neon party aesthetics**: the brand is a quiet speakeasy, not a strobe-lit club.
- **Fabricated social proof**: no invented user counts or fake testimonials; the product is seed-stage and honesty is part of trust.

## Design Principles

- **The lounge, not the storefront.** Every surface should read as a place you enter, never a thing you buy from.
- **James is the host.** The AI bartender carries the brand's warmth; lead with his voice, not feature lists.
- **Honest by default.** Show real content or none — never fake numbers or activity. Compliance and trust are features, not friction.
- **Pseudonymous intimacy.** Personal and warm without ever asking for a real identity.
- **Token-driven, dual-theme.** Light (Ivory Cream) and dark (Midnight Lounge) are both first-class; build on semantic tokens so every surface adapts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, including muted text and placeholders on tinted backgrounds.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: ambient FX (orbs, glare, sparkles) and entrance animations must degrade to static.
- Keyboard- and screen-reader-navigable auth flows; the age gate and auth are the critical paths.
- Dual light/dark themes must both preserve contrast and hierarchy.
